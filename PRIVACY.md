# SkyDock privacy policy

_Last updated: 14 October 2026_

SkyDock is a program that runs on your own computer. It has no account, no server of its own and no
analytics. **Nothing about you, your files or the people in them is sent to the developer.**

## What SkyDock reads

- **Camera cards and drives** you plug in: it lists and reads the videos and photos on them. It only
  reads them; a card is never changed unless you ask for files to be deleted from it.
- **Files you add** yourself, by choosing or dropping them.
- **What you type**: names of jumps, montages and passengers, and an email address when you write the
  email to a passenger.

The videos and photos, and the names and dates that go with them, can show and identify people. They
are used only to sort, prepare and hand over the files, on your computer.

## What SkyDock keeps, and where

Everything stays on the computer, in two places you can see and delete:

- **The work folder**, which you choose when you first open SkyDock: the originals, the copies and
  films it makes, and a record of the jumps (their times, names and what was done to them). The marks
  it finds in a clip's own motion data are kept there too.
- **The settings folder**, apart from the work: your choices, and the connection to your network
  storage — its address, the user name and the password. **The password is kept encrypted** and is
  used only to sign in to that storage.

## What leaves your computer, and when

SkyDock reaches out only for these, and only as described:

1. **Your own network storage.** When you connect a storage (a Synology NAS, for example) and upload,
   the files and the folders you chose are sent to **that storage, at the address you entered**. SkyDock
   can ask the storage for a share link; the link is made and held by your storage, not by SkyDock.
2. **The email to a passenger.** SkyDock sends no email itself. It prepares the text and opens a new
   message in Gmail in your browser, or in your computer's own mail program, with the address you typed.
   What happens next is between you and that mail provider.
3. **Looking for a new version.** When it opens, SkyDock asks the project's release page on GitHub
   whether a newer version exists. GitHub sees the request as it sees any download — your internet
   address and the program's version. A new version is installed only if you say yes.

There is nothing else: no usage statistics, no crash reports, no advertising, no tracking, and no
sharing with third parties by SkyDock.

## The people in your footage

If you film or photograph passengers and others, **you are the one who holds that footage**, and the
rules of the place where you work about consent, image rights and data protection apply to you. SkyDock
only makes it easier to keep the files where you meant them to be.

## Removing everything

Delete the work folder and the settings folder, then remove SkyDock. Files you uploaded stay on your
storage until you delete them there; SkyDock holds no copy of them anywhere else.

## Children

SkyDock is a tool for the people who run a dropzone. It is not meant for children and collects nothing
from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new text is published in this file with a new date at the top. The earlier
versions stay in the project's history.
