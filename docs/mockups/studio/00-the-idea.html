SkyDockVisual direction · v5
Studio

A professional tool for a day of footage.

SkyDock is an installed app, so it should look like one: like the editor and the file manager it sits next to on the desktop. A toolbar instead of a header, panes that meet on fine lines, a status bar along the bottom, and one typeface throughout.

The thumbnails carry the pictures. The interface around them stays neutral grey, with a single blue for selection and the next action. Every screen, button and rule is today's.

01
A window, not a page

SkyDock is installed and opens as a window of its own. The desktop draws the title bar; under it a toolbar, three panes side by side, and a status bar for the storage, what is copying or uploading, proxies and zoom.

02
One typeface

Inter for everything, with tabular figures so times and sizes line up. Weight, not size, makes the hierarchy. File names are in JetBrains Mono.

03
Grey, then blue

Zinc greys for surfaces and text. Blue marks the place you are on, the open jump, what is picked and the button that does the next step.

04
Status as badges

Local, processed and uploaded are small tinted badges in amber, violet and green, so a column of files reads like a report.
