Logbook

Every jump,
kept well.

Skydivers keep a logbook: one line per jump, the time, the place, a signature. SkyDock now looks like a well-made one. Warm paper, a serif for names and times, and the footage itself doing the colouring.

1
The footage leads

Jumps are shown by their own pictures, large and first. The interface around them stays quiet, so the sky is the colour on the screen.

2
Paper and sheets

The places sit on warm paper. The work sits on white sheets with soft edges. The open jump, montage or dropzone always has the sheet on the right.

3
A serif for what matters

Names, times and counts are set in Instrument Serif, like entries in a logbook. Everything read and pressed is Hanken Grotesk; sizes and file times are IBM Plex Mono.

4
One colour for what is next

Terracotta, the colour of a canopy, marks the open jump, what is picked and the next step. File states are a dot and a word, in ochre, slate and pine.
