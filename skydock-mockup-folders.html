Proposal — the board as a file browser. One screen, three panes, nothing to unfold.
  • Folders on the left, like any file manager: Cameras (the days, as copied off), Dropzones, Tandems, Storage. Each says how much of it is still to do.
  • Everything in the folder is on screen, grouped by jump, by day or as one list — one button each in the heading — with headers that stay put while you scroll. Files are always in the order shot. No day toggles, no folded jumps, no chips.
  • Filing is one click from a jump's own header (File to ▾), or a drag onto a folder. Naming a passenger happens in the inspector, without leaving the day.
  • The inspector on the right shows whatever is selected — a file, a jump, the whole folder — with its one next step. Click to look, double-click to open the cropper.
  • The next step is always in the same place: the folder's button at the top right, and the selection's in the inspector. What is left is listed above the files as clickable chips.
  • Keyboard as in a file manager: ↑↓ move, ⇧/⌘ pick, ↵ open, ⌫ send back, ⌘A all.
